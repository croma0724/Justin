Lesson1

Objectives:  display the time
 time():   		retrieve time elapsed from 1970 in seconds
 ctime_s():  	convert time in seconds to user friendly format. Sample:
	 			"Sun Apr 12 09:28:52 2026"

Getting time:
            time elapsed in seconds   
   time()   -----------------------> ctime_s() =>  "Sun Apr 12 09:28:52 2026"
